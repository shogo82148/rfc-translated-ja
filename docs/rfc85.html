Network Working Group meeting

RFC 85

Network Working Groupの会合

Abstract

概要

In line with the consensus of the Houston Network Working Group meeting in November, we will hold general Network Working Group meetings approximately every three months. The Spring and Fall meetings will be synchronized to the Joint Computer Conferences. The others will be moved around in order to promote exposure to the various sites and spread the burden of hosting.

11月にHoustonで開かれたNetwork Working Group会合での合意に沿って、Network Working Groupの全体会合をおよそ3か月ごとに開催します。春と秋の会合はJoint Computer Conferenceに合わせて開催します。それ以外の会合は、各サイトを知ってもらう機会を増やし、主催の負担を分散するために、開催地を持ち回りにします。
